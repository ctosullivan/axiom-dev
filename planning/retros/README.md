# Retrospectives

Retrospectives describe lessons, evidence, unexpected findings and
process overhead. They are historical records, not current status
summaries; see `planning/CONTEXT.md` for what happens next.

[Phase 1 Stages 1.0–1.5](phase-1-stages-1.0-through-1.5.md)
was written before formal owner completion and intentionally records
the then-current limitations. Phase 1 was subsequently marked
Completed by the owner on 2026-11-22, with qualifications preserved
in [the separate acceptance record](../../evidence/phase-1-owner-acceptance.md).
Do not rewrite retrospective observations to imply the limitations
did not exist.
